Most hiring pipelines still collapse at the same point.

Everyone looks strong on paper.
Few people get a real chance to show how they think.

AI made applications cleaner.
It did not make judgment easier.

Resumes get more polished.
Take-homes get more generic.
Live interviews stay expensive — and still uneven.

The teams moving faster right now are not interviewing more.
They are getting an earlier skill signal.

Something async.
Something comparable across candidates.
Something that surfaces reasoning, not just a finished answer.

~15 minutes of structured think-aloud work can change who reaches the hiring manager.
A real multi-block work sample can replace the take-home nobody has time to grade well.

Hard skills should be verified before they become calendar debt.

If you are scaling a team — or placing hard-skill roles for clients — curious how you currently prove fit before the interview loop.
