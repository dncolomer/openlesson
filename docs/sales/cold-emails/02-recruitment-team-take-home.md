Classic take-homes break in two places: they are too expensive for high-volume roles, and they still burn senior review time on premium ones. AI-polished PDFs also hide process — you only see the final paste.

**Self-Service Take-Home** is an open-ended, **multi-block** assignment candidates complete inside the product (discussion, diagrams, notes, multi-step work). You get the same reporting model we use for early screens:

- **Job-position ranking** on expected role performance
- **Per-applicant strengths and weaknesses** for selective human review
- One structured journey per role so the cohort is comparable

Two use cases teams care about:

1. **Volume roles** — make a real work sample viable where classic take-homes failed economics  
2. **High-profile offers** — lower cost and cycle time without lowering the bar
