Many agencies can source and present candidates — fewer can **prove hard skill** before the client burns interview slots. Resume screens and generic tests leave clients skeptical; custom take-homes are hard to productize and expensive to grade at scale.

We help agencies run **hard-skill verification** as a repeatable service:

**Self-Service Skill Check**  
Candidates complete a ~15-minute self-service think-aloud evaluation via private link. You (and your client) receive a **role ranking** and **strengths/weaknesses** per applicant — consistent bar per role, no live interviewer required. Hosted links or **API** into your workflow.

**Self-Service Take-Home**  
For deeper roles: multi-block open-ended assignments (discussion, diagrams, notes) with the same ranking + per-applicant reports — work samples that stay economical for volume placements and cleaner for senior searches.

What that means commercially:

- Stronger shortlists with **comparable, role-specific skill signal**  
- A clear verification story for clients who say “we need proof, not just profiles”  
- Less dependency on client-side senior engineers to grade every packet
